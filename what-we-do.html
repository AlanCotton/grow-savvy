What we do

A new age of growth.

Not another tool. Not another junior sales or marketing person.

Just senior expertise across every part of your climb, sharpened with AI that actually delivers.

The bold claim

We know, it's a bold claim.

Until now, growing a small business has meant one of two routes: hiring someone junior in-house, or handing it to an agency and getting someone junior working with you. Both options have upsides, but both also have significant downsides.
Grow Savvy is neither.

No juniors. Just senior operators who know how to build and grow businesses.

Partnering with business owners, we give them senior expertise across every part of their growth journey. Our small specialist team has founded and run several successful small businesses over decades. We're here to help you reach the summit, and avoid falling down the inevitable crevasses along the route.

Think of us as your NED, your mentor and an honest (sometimes too honest) confidant. And by working shoulder to shoulder, together we'll achieve the growth you want, and you'll be brilliant at it.

Having founded HCA, one of the first go-to-market consultancies in the UK, we know how to navigate a market full of false promises, so you don't waste your time, your money or, most importantly, your reputation finding out the hard way.

FAQ

Questions business owners ask us.

Who do you work with?

Owners of ambitious small and mid-sized B2B businesses who want to grow, and want senior people beside them while they do it.

Who will I actually work with?

Jane Heath and Alan Cotton, personally. Every engagement is shaped and delivered by them – you never get passed down the line to someone junior.

How many clients do you take on?

No more than five non-competing businesses at any one time, so every business owner gets the attention and support they deserve.

Do you build AI tools?

AI is part of our growth support: we help you choose, set up and adopt the right tools around how your business works. For bespoke AI builds, our sister company The Taylor, also part of Chameleon Brands, is the specialist.

What does the first conversation cost?

Nothing. The first conversation is on us – drop us a line or book a 30-minute call.
